GUIDED TRAJECTORY ZIPS · in plain English
========================================
Full detail lives in each zip's DATA_NOTES.txt. One-line map:

  Zip A  Weather time series     -> design shelter against cold + dust
  Zip B  Photos + trip logs      -> navigate / schedule transport safely
  Zip C  Resource daily totals   -> keep air, water, food, power alive

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Track A · track-a-architecture-emars.zip
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What: Spreadsheet-style weather for one Mars settlement site.
Type: Numbers over time (CSV / parquet) -- temp, wind, pressure, dust,
      heating load for 100 people. Hourly for ~2 years (730 sols).
      Storm days flagged (sols 180-260).
Useful for: Habitats / architecture -- size walls, insulation, or a
      "seal the bunker restaurant" rule when it freezes or storms.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Track B · track-b-vehicles-ai4mars.zip
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What: (1) Rover photos with labels for soil / bedrock / sand / rock.
      (2) Trip timetable CSV -- thousands of runs over 730 sols for 100 people.
Type: Images + masks (vision) and a logistics table (tabular).
Useful for: Vehicles / mobility -- score safe paths and energy for a rover
      or Mars train, including dust storms and battery wear.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Track C · track-c-life-support-hre.zip
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What: Daily life-support log for 100 people across 730 days.
Type: Numbers table (CSV) -- O2, CO2, water, food, waste, solar,
      greenhouse ventilation, cabin CO2 proxy.
Useful for: Life support -- forecast shortages or run greenhouse air when
      a dust storm kills the sunlight.
